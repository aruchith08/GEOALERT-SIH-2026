2. TWI & SPI DIMENSIONAL & HYDROLOGICAL AUDIT
================================================================================
A. Specific Catchment Area (As) Formulation:
   - In hydrology, Specific Catchment Area (As) represents the upslope drainage area
     divided by contour width (unit width b across flow line).
   - Units: [Area] / [Width] = m^2 / m = meters (m).
   - Local 30m Grid Formulation:
       As = (N_accum + 1) * cell_width
     where N_accum is the count of upstream cells draining into the point, and
     cell_width = dx = 30.87 * cos(lat) ≈ 27.9m.
   - For a primary ridge cell with N_accum = 0 (only itself), As = 1 * dx = 27.9m.

B. Topographic Wetness Index (TWI):
   - Formula (Moore et al., 1991 / Beven & Kirkby, 1979):
       TWI = ln( As / tan(beta) )
     where beta is the slope gradient in radians, and tan(beta) is clamped to >= 0.001
     to prevent division-by-zero on flat terrain.
   - Range across Meghalaya Landslides: 3.32 to 10.89 (Median: 5.38, Mean: 5.55).
   - Dimensionality: Dimensionless ratio inside natural logarithm (meters / dimensionless).
   - Audit Result: PASS (Dimensionally and physically consistent).

C. Stream Power Index (SPI):
   - Formula (Moore et al., 1991):
       SPI = As * tan(beta)
   - Range across Meghalaya Landslides: 0.39m to 1,914.60m (Median: 29.60m, Mean: 71.47m).
   - Dimensionality: Meters (m).
   - Physical Meaning: Directly proportional to stream discharge and local hydraulic gradient,
     indexing overland flow shear stress and gully scouring potential.
   - Audit Result: PASS (Shared hydrological basis with TWI).

D. HydroSHEDS 15-Arcsecond Integration Note:
   - WWF/HydroSHEDS/15ACC is an independent global hydrological flow accumulation product
     at ~450m resolution.
   - When used in GEE (gee/01_srtm_features.js), each cell value represents the number of
     15-arcsec upstream cells. Resampling to 30m enables regional basin-scale contributing area
     estimation, while local 30m D8 routing reflects local slope micro-catchments. Both
     methodologies are explicitly documented in the GEE script and provenance reports.

3. CURVATURE FORMULATION & SIGN CONVENTION AUDIT
================================================================================
The high-order terrain curvatures follow the analytical 2nd-order polynomial formulations
of Zevenbergen & Thorne (1987) / Moore et al. (1991):

A. Metric Coordinate Scaling:
   - Latitude spacing: dy = 30.872 meters
   - Longitude spacing: dx = 30.872 * cos(lat) ≈ 27.9 meters

B. Partial Derivatives:
   - 1st Order (Horn 8-neighbor / Zevenbergen-Thorne):
       p = dz/dx,  q = dz/dy
   - 2nd Order:
       r = d2z/dx2 = (z_E - 2*z_C + z_W) / dx^2
       t = d2z/dy2 = (z_N - 2*z_C + z_S) / dy^2
       s = d2z/dxdy = ((z_NE - z_NW) - (z_SE - z_SW)) / (4 * dx * dy)

C. Plan Curvature (K_plan):
   - Formula: K_plan = - (q^2 * r - 2*p*q*s + p^2 * t) / (p^2 + q^2)^1.5
   - Units: 1/m (inverse meters)
   - Sign Convention:
       * Negative (< 0): Convergent contour lines (hollows, gullies, valley flanks)
       * Positive (> 0): Divergent contour lines (ridges, spurs, noses)
       * Zero (= 0): Planar slope
   - Observed Range in Meghalaya: -0.3827 to +0.0748 (Mean: +0.0004, Std: 0.0213)
   - Audit Result: PASS (Properly zero-centered, units explicit).

D. Profile Curvature (K_prof):
   - Formula: K_prof = - (p^2 * r + 2*p*q*s + q^2 * t) / ((p^2 + q^2) * (1 + p^2 + q^2)^1.5)
   - Units: 1/m (inverse meters)
   - Sign Convention:
       * Negative (< 0): Convex slope profile (downslope accelerating flow)
       * Positive (> 0): Concave slope profile (downslope decelerating/depositional flow)
       * Zero (= 0): Linear slope
   - Observed Range in Meghalaya: -0.0196 to +0.0196 (Mean: -0.0002, Std: 0.0041)
   - Audit Result: PASS (Properly zero-centered, units explicit).
